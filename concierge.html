02 / BEFORE YOU APPLY

Verify before you move.

Use this quick check on the original provider website.

  1. Confirm the employer.The website belongs to the actual employer, department or programme owner.
  2. Check the closing date.The application window is still open today.
  3. Read eligibility carefully.Age, location, qualifications and documents match the official notice.
  4. Follow the original application route.Use the provider's own portal. Never pay someone to secure a job.
