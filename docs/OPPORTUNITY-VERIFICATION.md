# BACCU Opportunity Verification Standard

This standard governs every opportunity BACCU publishes in Ambition Unleashed.

## 1. Coverage order

1. Actonville, Benoni and the wider City of Ekurhuleni.
2. The rest of Gauteng.
3. All other South African provinces.

National opportunities must never be excluded because they are outside Gauteng.

## 2. Approved source hierarchy

Use the provider responsible for the opportunity wherever possible:

- National and provincial government departments.
- Municipalities and public entities.
- SETAs and accredited training providers, with the provider's own confirmation.
- Universities and TVET colleges.
- Employers and official graduate-programme portals.
- NYDA, SEDFA and other public youth-development institutions.

SA Youth and other opportunity networks may be used as discovery channels. Confirm the underlying opportunity on the employer or programme owner's official source before marking it verified.

A source directory, social post, search snippet or aggregator is not proof that an application is open.

## 3. Required fields for a live listing

A live listing must include all applicable fields:

- Opportunity title and provider.
- Opportunity type.
- Province, municipality or nationwide eligibility.
- Eligibility requirements, including age, study level and residency where stated.
- Application status: Open, Closed, or Not confirmed.
- Official closing date, only when the provider states one.
- Required documents or application steps.
- Direct official application or information URL.
- Date last checked.
- BACCU reviewer or review record.

Do not publish a listing as Open if the official source cannot confirm the active intake.

## 4. Status and expiry rules

- **Open:** the official provider confirms applications are currently being accepted.
- **Closed:** the provider confirms the intake has closed or the stated deadline has passed.
- **Not confirmed:** the source is unavailable, unclear, undated or does not confirm the current intake.

Closed and unconfirmed listings must not appear in an Open opportunities view. Never invent deadlines, stipends, funding amounts, eligibility or selection outcomes.

Recheck every active listing during the weekly review. Recheck urgent deadlines more frequently when practical. Record changes and remove expired calls from the active view.

## 5. Local priority and national reach

Display verified Ekurhuleni opportunities first, then other Gauteng opportunities, then opportunities from the rest of South Africa. Label the location clearly. A national programme may still have province-specific placements or eligibility.

## 6. Applicant safety and privacy

- Never charge young people to access BACCU listings.
- Warn users about recruitment scams and requests for payment.
- Link directly to official provider application pages.
- BACCU must not collect identity documents, CVs, bank details or application credentials in the first release.
- Do not submit applications or create accounts on behalf of applicants.
- Do not imply a partnership, endorsement or guaranteed result.

## 7. First-release scope

The first release is a lightweight, mobile-first directory and skills resource. It does not provide automated vacancy feeds, applicant accounts, AI-generated application submissions or a recruitment service. The weekly BACCU research task supports editorial verification; it does not automatically publish data to the website.

**Trust before reach. Accuracy before volume.**
